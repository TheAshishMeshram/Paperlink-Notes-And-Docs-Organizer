Getting Started
Follow these steps to get the most out of Paperlink Organizer.
1

Upload Your Files

Click the + Upload Files button in the top-right, or simply drag and drop files anywhere onto the upload zone. You can upload multiple files at once — there's no limit on file count, only on your browser's available localStorage (~5–10 MB typical). A progress bar will track each upload.

2

Browse by Category

Use the left sidebar to filter by category — Documents, Images, Code, Video, and more. Files are auto-categorized based on their extension the moment they're uploaded. You can also view Recent (last 7 days) or your Starred items.

3

Add Tags and Notes

Click any file to open its detail panel. You can add searchable tags (press Enter after each tag) and write a personal annotation in the notes field. These are saved automatically to localStorage alongside the file metadata.

4

Star Important Files

Click the ☆ icon on any file card to star it. Starred files are accessible instantly from the Starred section in the sidebar. Stars persist across sessions.

5

Download by Category

Each section heading has a Download as ZIP button. Hover over any category in the sidebar to reveal a quick ⬇ button. Or use Export All in the header to download every file organized into category sub-folders inside one ZIP.

6

Bulk Operations

Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) and click multiple files to select them. A bulk action bar appears at the top — you can star, download as ZIP, or delete all selected files at once.

7

Write Quick Notes

Press Ctrl+X or click ✏️ Quick Note to open the inline note editor. Give it a title and write your content — it's saved as a .note file in the Notes category and is fully searchable.

8

Storage Limits

Browser localStorage is typically limited to 5–10 MB depending on your browser. The storage bar in the sidebar shows how much you've used. For large media files, download and remove them after archiving to keep storage clear. Large files may trigger a quota warning.

⚠️ Important: Paperlink stores files as base64 in localStorage. Clearing your browser data or using Incognito/Private mode will erase your files. For permanent storage, always use Export All to keep a backup ZIP on your disk.
